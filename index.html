How the edges get crispy

Side view on top, seen from above underneath. All three drawn to the same scale.

  1. Ball

    A ball of chicken or mutton goes onto a hot griddle.

  2. Smash

    We press it down hard until it is thin and flat.

  3. Crisp

    The thin edge fries against the steel and turns lacy and crisp.

Then a flip, melted cheese, and into a toasted bun.
